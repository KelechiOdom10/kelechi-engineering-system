# Step 5: design direction

Skip with a reason when the product has no UI, or when an existing design system already decides the result.

Load `frontend-design-direction` and follow it with `../../../templates/frontend-design-brief.md`. Pull detail from the installed `better-*` skills (layout, typography, colour) when they are available.

## Frontier

- The visual direction in one phrase, and why it serves the core job.
- Content and action hierarchy for the primary screen.
- Type, colour roles, radius, density, and motion character.
- What the product must not look like (from "Not this" in `docs/product.md`).
- State rules: loading, empty, error, permission-denied.

The greenfield visual defaults in the standard's `frontend` reference are the recommendation unless the direction argues otherwise.

## `docs/design.md`

One screen: direction, hierarchy, tokens and their roles, state rules, and the breakpoints that matter.
